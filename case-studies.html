Selected Work

Turning operational challenges into structured solutions.

These case studies describe selected work across learning-platform operations, institutional partnerships and analytics workflows. They focus on the problem, the approach and the business relevance, while respecting data privacy and the limits of available evidence.

Operations Learning Technology Reporting

01 — Learning Management System Operations & Analytics

The challenge

As a learning platform expanded, consistent learner records, usable reporting and visibility into engagement became increasingly important for operational decision-making.

My approach

  • Supported LMS operations and coordination across stakeholders.
  • Worked with learner and institutional data to identify inconsistencies, including variations in college names and contact records.
  • Used a workflow involving MySQL, Excel, Python and Power BI to support data preparation, analysis and reporting.
  • Explored analytics approaches for learner segmentation and engagement insights to help inform follow-up and programme planning.
Reported platform scale 10,000+ active learners
Earlier baseline Approximately 2,000 learners
Workflow MySQL → Excel → Python → Power BI

Business relevance

More consistent records and structured reporting can improve operational visibility, reduce avoidable data-cleaning effort and support better-informed programme decisions.

Business Operations Institutional Partnerships Programme Coordination

02 — Institutional Partnership Programme

The challenge

Colleges often need connected technology-learning solutions rather than isolated activities. Programme design must account for student needs, job-market relevance, trainer availability and laboratory readiness.

My approach

  • Supported institutional outreach, college visits and seminars.
  • Contributed to coordination around technology programmes and institutional requirements.
  • Helped connect programme discussions with factors such as student numbers, relevant technology domains, trainer capacity and lab requirements.
  • Worked across stakeholders to support follow-up and programme execution.
Outreach activity 80+ college visits and seminars
Reported programme context 200+ MoUs across the wider organisation
Focus Institutional coordination and technology education

Business relevance

A structured partnership approach can help align institutional requirements with programme delivery, technical resources and longer-term education objectives.

Data Operations Recruitment Workflow Data Quality

03 — Candidate Data & Screening Workflow Support

The challenge

Large candidate datasets require consistent screening criteria, organised records and traceable outputs so teams can review information more efficiently.

My approach

  • Supported workflows involving candidate records and screening data.
  • Worked with structured outputs in spreadsheets, including candidate references and resume links.
  • Worked with a stated 55% screening threshold as part of the workflow context.
  • Considered data consistency and the importance of human review, especially where automated screening may miss relevant candidates.
Reported dataset scale 105,000+ registered or eligible candidates
Screening context 55% threshold
Tools and outputs Excel, Google Sheets and MySQL

Business relevance

Organised candidate data can make screening outputs easier to review and help teams maintain a more consistent process. A threshold alone does not establish screening accuracy or hiring outcomes.

Prototype Web Development Blockchain Concepts

04 — Academic Healthcare Records Prototype

Project overview

An academic prototype exploring a digital workflow for healthcare records, with an emphasis on access flow and decentralised-storage concepts.

Technology explored

  • React and Vite for the user interface.
  • Node.js and Express for backend services.
  • MongoDB for application data.
  • Pinata IPFS, MetaMask and Polygon Mumbai test-network concepts.
  • Patient-code and OTP-style access flow concepts.

This is an academic prototype, not a verified production healthcare system. It should not be interpreted as clinically validated or suitable for storing real patient information.
