CASE STUDY 01 / DATA & REPORTING

LMS analytics and learner engagement

Turning platform data into more usable operational reporting.

10,000+ Reported learner scale

01 — The challenge

As the learning platform expanded, reporting depended on data being consistent, usable, and available for analysis. Inconsistent college names and contact details made comparisons and tracking harder.

02 — My contribution

  • Supported data cleaning and standardisation.
  • Worked across learner and institutional records.
  • Helped organise reporting for programme and engagement review.
  • Connected operational questions with analytical workflows.

03 — Tools and workflow

A practical workflow connecting data storage, preparation, analysis, and reporting.

MySQL→ Excel→ Python→ Power BI

Relevant tools included Pandas and NumPy for data handling, alongside visualisation and analysis tools used during exploration.

04 — Outcome and significance

The platform's reported scale increased from an earlier baseline of approximately 2,000 learners to more than 10,000 over roughly eight months. This is a platform-level figure, not a claim that my work alone caused the growth. Better organised data supported more consistent reporting and analysis.

The scale figures are user-reported aggregates. A verified month-by-month dataset and independently measured impact are not claimed here.
CASE STUDY 02 / PROGRAM OPERATIONS

Institutional Partnership Program

Connecting institutional needs with technology education programmes.

200+ Reported wider MoU activity

01 — The challenge

Colleges often needed a coordinated solution spanning technical training, internships, faculty development, labs, soft skills, and exposure to industry. Treating each requirement as an isolated service could make programme planning fragmented.

02 — My contribution

  • Supported college engagement and requirement discussions.
  • Helped coordinate programme and institutional information.
  • Worked with team members on delivery-related planning.
  • Contributed to the wider operations and outreach workflow.

03 — Planning considerations

  • Student and trainer counts.

04 — Outcome and significance

The wider organisation reported more than 200 MoUs across Maharashtra-related institutional partnership activity. This reflects the broader programme context and does not mean I personally secured or signed every agreement.

MoUs can differ in status and scope. This figure should not be interpreted as 200 independently verified, fully implemented programmes.
CASE STUDY 03 / DATA WORKFLOW

Recruitment data and screening support

Organising large candidate datasets for review and follow-up.

105,000+ Candidate records in scope

01 — The challenge

Large candidate datasets require consistent record organisation, screening criteria, and traceable outputs. Reviewing information across multiple records and files can become difficult without a structured workflow.

02 — My contribution

  • Supported candidate-data organisation and screening workflows.
  • Worked with structured spreadsheet outputs and resume links.
  • Supported workflow traceability with internal data storage.
  • Helped make records easier for the relevant team to review.

03 — Tools and process

Candidate records→ Screening workflow→ Review sheets

Workflow context included Excel or Google Sheets for review outputs and MySQL for internal storage. A 55% screening threshold was used in the described workflow.

04 — Outcome and significance

The workflow covered a reported pool of more than 105,000 registered or eligible candidate records. The figure is not a count of job applications, successful hires, or independently verified screening outcomes.

The threshold describes a workflow setting, not proof of hiring accuracy, fairness, or model performance. Candidate records and personal information are intentionally excluded from this portfolio.
